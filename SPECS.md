## PROJECT PURPOSE

Enable a deaf person in Portugal to follow and participate in conversations with friends in a crowded bar through readable live captions that identify who said what.

## PROJECT REQUIREMENTS

- Provide the experience through a webpage usable on participants' phones.
- Support multilingual transcription, including Portuguese as spoken in Portugal, with low delay suitable for following a live conversation.
- Let participants create or join a shared conversation room, with QR codes providing access to the webpage and room invitations.
- Stream participants' microphone audio to a server and share the resulting captions with all phones in the room, associated with the correct participant.
- Present captions in a user experience that enables the reader to follow the group conversation, including overlapping speech.
- Support multiple participant phones; prefer a single phone capture option when it can achieve a usable experience.
